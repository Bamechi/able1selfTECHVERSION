# Able1Self Revision - September 22, 2026

## Updated
- Shared light/dark mode on the landing page and member portal, remembered on this browser.
- Monochrome materials, quieter glass surfaces, larger readable text, consistent focus states, and reduced-motion support.
- Device imagery based on the actual member interface, with synthetic sample profile information.
- Overall and stage progress bars, expandable A/B/L/E program sections, and clearer question transitions.
- Personality preference heading appears once per group. The existing 52-question ABLE reflection stays in Analyze; it is not the official MBTI or 16Personalities assessment.
- Birth fields have readable contrast. Changing a location clears its old coordinates. Invalid dates, coordinates, time zones, and skipped daylight-saving times return no chart. One independent reference case matches; an individual's reported rising-sign discrepancy still requires their exact verified birth data to reproduce.
- Embark milestones generate a target plan once after E1-E3 are complete. Dates follow the selected 90-day, six-, nine-, or twelve-month horizon. Existing plan items are preserved.
- Profile printing uses stage divisions, readable typography, and clean birth-detail labels.
- Messaging combines a shared member board, an opt-in public introduction, and participant-only direct messages. Private questionnaires, birth details, and account email addresses are not exposed in the directory. Existing private legacy posts are not published automatically.
- AI Guide supports real profile-aware conversation and saved history instead of preset replies. It requires explicit member consent and a server-side OPENAI_API_KEY; no key is currently connected. It shows an honest unavailable state until configured. OPENAI_MODEL is optional. Raw birth fields and individual personality-test responses are excluded from the provider context.

## Preserved

- Business-foundation checkboxes and the independent personality reflection.
- The separate fashion Members Only dashboard remains absent from ABLE navigation; its existing source and data are retained.
